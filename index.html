Our Story

More Than a Meal.

Ember & Plate began with a simple idea: that a great restaurant should feel unhurried. In our open kitchen in the heart of Lekki, our chefs work over live fire and slow-cooked technique, pairing bold, smoke-driven flavour with the kind of quiet, attentive service that lets a meal breathe.

Every plate that leaves our pass is built around three things — the honesty of the ingredient, the precision of the cook, and the warmth of the room it's served in. We source produce from growers across the Southwest, work closely with local fishermen for the day's catch, and change our menu with the seasons rather than the calendar.

Whether you're joining us for a quiet dinner for two or gathering the whole table for a celebration, our aim stays the same — to give you an evening worth returning for.
